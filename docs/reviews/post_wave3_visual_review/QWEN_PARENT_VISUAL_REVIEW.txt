HERO_FINDINGS:
The hero section lacks a clear focal point. The greeting and child-context selector are visually disconnected. It should clearly state the greeting, followed immediately by the active child's selector and a single, high-priority status summary to anchor the parent's mental model.
KPI_FINDINGS:
KPI cards suffer from uniform visual weight. The primary metric lacks typographic dominance. Delta indicators (up/down arrows or trends) are inconsistently placed or colored, reducing immediate scanability.
GRID_FINDINGS:
The grid lacks disciplined gutters. There is visible misalignment between the sidebar edge and the main content cards, and inconsistent horizontal spacing between the 3-column and 2-column card rows, breaking the sense of a structured layout.
TYPOGRAPHY_FINDINGS:
Font weights are too uniform. Persian script requires careful weight tuning; body text appears too close in weight to headings, destroying the information hierarchy. Numerals are not consistently localized or tabularly aligned, harming readability.
COLOR_FINDINGS:
The palette is noisy. Multiple competing accent colors are used for different widgets, violating the "calm" requirement. Stick to a single primary brand color for actions/highlights, neutral grays for structural elements, and semantic colors (red/green) strictly for status indicators.
SIDEBAR_TOPBAR_FINDINGS:
The topbar is cluttered. The sidebar active state lacks a strong, distinct visual indicator in RTL (e.g., it may incorrectly use a left border accent instead of right). The topbar search, notification icons, and profile avatar lack clear visual grouping and adequate breathing room.
CTA_FINDINGS:
Primary and secondary CTAs lack sufficient contrast in visual weight. They appear to use similar border styles or background fills, making the "next best action" ambiguous for the parent.
RTL_BIDI_FINDINGS:
Mixed content (Persian text with Latin numbers or English terms) causes directional ambiguity. Chevrons and directional icons (like arrows) are occasionally pointing in LTR directions instead of being properly mirrored for RTL navigation.
WASTED_SPACE_FINDINGS:
There is excessive vertical whitespace between the hero section and the first row of KPI cards, while the internal padding of the KPI cards themselves is too cramped, creating an unbalanced and unprofessional rhythm.

The interface feels like an off-the-shelf admin template. It lacks custom micro-interactions, refined border radii consistency, and the subtle, deliberate spacing that defines a premium, highly polished product.
