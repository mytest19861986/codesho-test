HERO_FINDINGS:
The hero section dominates above-the-fold space with heavy brand saturation and decorative glassmorphism. It mimics a promotional landing page rather than a functional, reassuring workspace for a parent. The high visual weight forces critical diagnostic data below the primary viewport fold.

KPI_FINDINGS:
The 4 KPI cards lack internal hierarchy. Radial progress rings consume vertical height without increasing readability. The use of emojis damages the platform's credibility, and the cards do not indicate which metrics are healthy versus those requiring parental support.

GRID_FINDINGS:
The 1440px desktop grid lacks vertical unity. The 4-column KPI strip does not align cleanly with the 3-column middle zone or the 3-column bottom layout. Gutters vary between 16px and 24px, producing uneven margins and orphaned white patches at the bottom of cards.

TYPOGRAPHY_FINDINGS:
Vazirmatn weights are overused at the bold and black end of the spectrum. Subtitles and metadata compete with primary numerical figures. While Persian numerals are applied, the line-heights on multi-line text blocks feel tight, impairing quick scanning.

COLOR_FINDINGS:
The palette leans too heavily into dark purple and saturated violet surfaces. For an "Information-Rich but Calm" parent interface, backgrounds should be grounded in clean neutrals (e.g., neutral-50/100) with color reserved strictly for meaningful system states (success, warning, informational).
