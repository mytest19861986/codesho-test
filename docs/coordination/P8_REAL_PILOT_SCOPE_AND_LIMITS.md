# P8 Real Pilot Scope and Limits

## 1. Controlled Scope Envelope & Governance Limits
- **REAL_PILOT_LIMITS**: `TBD_BY_MANAGER` (Exclusive Human Manager Authority)
- **PROPOSED_SYNTHETIC_REHEARSAL_LIMITS** (Non-binding baseline proposal for technical rehearsal only):
  * Proposed Organizations: 1 organization (candidate baseline)
  * Proposed Maximum Active Students: 50 students (synthetic load boundary)
  * Proposed Maximum Duration: 14 calendar days from activation token consumption
- **GEOGRAPHIC_AND_REGULATORY_BOUNDARY**: Domestic jurisdiction only, governed by child protection and digital privacy mandates.

## 2. Permitted Capabilities (Zero Live Risk)
1. Formative, non-comparative learning activities.
2. Self-paced lesson reading and conceptual progress tracking.
3. Private formative feedback from authenticated mentors.
4. Intra-individual progress visualization (growth against self-baseline only).

## 3. Explicitly Forbidden Capabilities (Locked Out)
- **NO_STUDENT_RANKING**: Strict prohibition of percentiles, competitive scores, leaderboards, or relative cohort grading.
- **NO_LIVE_PAYMENTS**: Payment gateways and financial transaction modules remain disabled/mocked.
- **NO_DIRECT_COMMUNICATION_OUTSIDE_PLATFORM**: Direct SMS, marketing emails, or external messaging are disabled.
- **NO_UNVETTED_THIRD_PARTY_INTEGRATIONS**: External AI calls at runtime without individual signed ADRs remain prohibited.
