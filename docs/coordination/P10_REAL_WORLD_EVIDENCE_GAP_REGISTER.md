# P10 Real-World Evidence Gap Register

## 1. Principle of Real-World Divergence
Synthetic rehearsal success proves system logic and deterministic constraints, but does not replicate the stochastic complexities of real humans, live telecom networks, and adversarial conditions. This register transparently records all dimensions unproven in live operational environments.

## 2. Unproven Real-World Dimensions

### A. Human & Behavioral Gaps
1. **Guardian Consent Comprehension**: Real parents may misinterpret, delay, or contest digital consent receipts. Behavioral friction cannot be modeled purely by synthetic fixtures.
2. **Minor User Compliance**: Children's actual navigation patterns, accidental inputs, and session sharing behaviors remain unobserved in a live classroom setting.
3. **School Administrator Adherence**: Staff may attempt out-of-band onboarding or bypass procedures if UX friction is perceived as high.

### B. Network & Infrastructure Gaps
4. **Domestic Telecom Gateway Latency (SMS)**: Domestic SMS aggregators (e.g. Kavenegar) frequently experience queuing delays, filtering of OTP messages, or transient carrier drops.
5. **National Internet Network Jitter**: Domestic routing fluctuations (Intranet/Internet peering) and edge mobile carrier disconnections have only been evaluated via synthetic mocks.
6. **Concurrent School Load**: Simultaneous morning login spikes across multiple real institutional subnets have not been load-tested against live production infrastructure.

### C. Operational & Support Gaps
7. **Parent Incident Volume**: Real-time ticket triaging for account access, consent revocation, or student progress inquiries is untested under real operational staffing.
8. **Crisis Communication SLAs**: Escalation pathways from an automated Kill-Switch trip to parent SMS notifications remain unpracticed with real contact numbers.
